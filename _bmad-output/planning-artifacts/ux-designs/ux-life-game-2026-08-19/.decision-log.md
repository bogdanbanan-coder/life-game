## 2026-08-22 — UX-A1 same-frame input versus scheduled generation

- **Resolution:** Compatible with the GDD, `project-context.md`, and the architecture's deterministic fixed-step model.
- **Authoritative frame order:** scheduled simulation steps → accepted same-frame input commands → rendering.
- **User-facing behavior:** While Running, an accepted Live or Die edit mutates the current field and is visible in that frame. The UX makes no promise that the edit affects a generation already processed earlier in that frame.
- **Boundary rule:** The exact wall-clock timer-boundary-to-frame assignment is an implementation detail; reproducibility is verified through the ordered phase trace, not a user-visible race at the boundary.
- **Non-goals:** Do not buffer edits specially and do not require the user to pause before editing.
- **Affected artifacts:** `game-architecture.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, and the UX-A1 acceptance criteria in `epics.md`.

## 2026-08-24 — UX-A2 delayed-frame catch-up policy

- **Decision:** Adopt the architecture's bounded catch-up policy: one main-loop iteration executes at most four due simulation generations total, then discards additional whole-generation timing backlog while retaining any sub-interval accumulator remainder.
- **Rendering and input:** Intermediate catch-up generations are not rendered. Accepted input is processed after the catch-up batch and is visible in the render that follows; it cannot affect generations already executed in that batch.
- **User-facing behavior:** Remove the `Running slower than interval.` promise and the claim that every delayed interval is retained until cadence recovers. Do not show a replacement slow-simulation status message.
- **Correctness boundary:** Every generation actually executed is complete and consumes the preceding completed field state. Discarded timing intervals are not simulated later, so long stalls sacrifice wall-time fidelity rather than mathematical sequencing.
- **Input boundary:** After the catch-up batch, the application samples the input state exposed by raylib, translates it into typed commands, and executes accepted commands before rendering. Catch-up adds no project-owned input retention or replay queue; input not exposed at that sampling phase can be accepted only in a later iteration if it remains observable.
- **Affected artifacts:** `game-architecture.md`, `project-context.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, the UX-A2 acceptance criteria in `epics.md`, and the implementation-readiness report.

## 2026-08-24 — UX-A4 presentation ownership and Start Screen

- **Decision:** Make the Start Screen the `AppScreen` that owns the session browser and Settings panel. A session card contains an inert field-preview picture; selecting it opens the interactive Field Screen. The Field Screen owns the upper-right Toolbar, and the Toolbar opens the one application-wide Bank panel.
- **Settings:** The Settings panel uses a two-column table with setting names on the left and editable values on the right. It exposes Field width and Field height in cells plus the generation interval. Width and height are defaults for new sessions; existing session dimensions remain fixed.
- **Dialog ownership:** `NameDialog` handles session/figure names. `ConfirmationDialog` is the explicit Confirm/Cancel modal for destructive deletes. `ErrorDialog`, reusable text/numeric fields, and `StatusMessage` own failure, validation, and non-modal feedback respectively.
- **Rationale:** This removes the architecture's ambiguous screen/component ownership while preserving the existing session, Field, Bank, and persistence flows.
- **Affected artifacts:** `EXPERIENCE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `game-architecture.md`, `gdd.md`, the UX-A4 acceptance criteria in `epics.md`, `project-context.md`, and the implementation-readiness report.

## 2026-08-24 — UX-A5 synchronous feedback and Bank pause

- **Decision:** Accept the architecture's synchronous main-thread execution for session, Bank, and preview operations. Busy feedback is limited to a named pre-operation state when useful and a success/failure result after the call; the whole window may block during the call, with no live-progress or partial-interactivity promise.
- **Bank behavior:** Opening Bank pauses the active Field and clears accumulated simulation time while keeping Bank controls interactive. Closing or canceling without placement selects Live and starts a fresh interval; a selected figure remains paused in Bank Preview until Resume or invalid-placement handling resolves it.
- **Rationale:** This removes the contradiction between UX feedback and the no-background-thread architecture while preserving deliberate, safe figure selection and placement.
- **Affected artifacts:** `EXPERIENCE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `game-architecture.md`, `gdd.md`, the UX-A5 acceptance criteria in `epics.md`, `project-context.md`, and the implementation-readiness report.

## 2026-08-24 — UX-A6 persistence failure categories

- **Decision:** Replace session-index and damaged-file language with the architecture's SQLite failure categories. A database-open, schema, or migration failure is fatal at startup: show a specific startup Error dialog, do not open the Start Screen or create a replacement database, and exit after acknowledgment.
- **Record behavior:** If the database opens but one session or Bank figure fails validation, preserve the database, keep valid records usable, and show the affected session card or Bank row disabled when its identity is available. Selecting the damaged item shows a specific error; no record is automatically repaired, overwritten, deleted, or replaced with empty data.
- **Rationale:** The UI now reflects the actual storage topology and distinguishes loss of the storage service from corruption isolated to one persisted record.
- **Affected artifacts:** `EXPERIENCE.md`, `game-architecture.md`, `gdd.md`, the UX-A6 acceptance criteria in `epics.md`, `project-context.md`, and the implementation-readiness report.

## 2026-08-24 — UX-A7 global Settings

- **Decision:** Field width, Field height, and generation interval are stored in one global Settings record edited from the Start Screen Settings panel. There is no per-session Settings layer for these values.
- **Activation:** Width and height are global defaults used only when creating a new session; existing session dimensions remain fixed. The current global generation interval is used when a session is created or opened after Save, and that transition begins a fresh interval.
- **Rationale:** This matches the user's Settings-menu model while preserving the fixed-dimension session invariant.
- **Affected artifacts:** `EXPERIENCE.md`, `game-architecture.md`, `gdd.md`, the UX-A7 acceptance criteria in `epics.md`, `project-context.md`, and the implementation-readiness report.

## 2026-08-24 — UX-A8 platform scaling and input

- **Decision:** Specify all UI, pointer-target, and Field presentation dimensions in logical client pixels. Body text is 16 logical px, compact/numeric text is 14 logical px, and pointer targets are at least 32×32 logical px.
- **Window contract:** Use a 1280×720 logical-pixel initial client area and a 960×540 minimum supported logical viewport. Resizing remains optional; added space belongs to the Field viewport while token-sized controls remain stable.
- **Input contract:** Apply OS DPI scaling once and normalize platform/framebuffer pointer coordinates to logical client coordinates before UI hit testing, camera conversion, or Field cell mapping. Verify the 4-logical-pixel grid threshold and pointer mapping on macOS and Linux.
- **Rationale:** This gives the platform implementation one authoritative scale and coordinate path while preserving the mouse-first UX and exact cell mapping.
- **Affected artifacts:** `EXPERIENCE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `game-architecture.md`, `gdd.md`, the UX-A8 acceptance criteria in `epics.md`, `project-context.md`, and the implementation-readiness report.

## 2026-08-24 — UX-A9 camera boundary and out-of-field rendering

- **Decision:** Clamp camera movement so the viewport always shows at least one in-field cell. Exact centering when the Field is smaller than the viewport remains implementation-owned.
- **Rendering contract:** Visible space outside the finite Field uses the gray `{colors.out-of-field}` fill (`#808080`). It is presentation-only: not a third cell state, not simulated, not editable, and not persisted as field data; a saved session preview may include its gray pixels. In-field dead cells remain black and in-field live cells remain white.
- **Rationale:** The player can inspect the finite boundary without losing the Field or confusing outside space with dead cells.
- **Affected artifacts:** `EXPERIENCE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `game-architecture.md`, `gdd.md`, the UX-A9 acceptance criteria in `epics.md`, `project-context.md`, and the implementation-readiness report.

## 2026-08-25 — UX-A11 implementation-readiness decisions resolved

- **Interaction:** Zoom anchors on the pointer cell or viewport center outside the Field; paint interpolates samples; Move/Highlight capture until release; staged figures use a top-left cell anchor and retain the last in-window preview.
- **Settings:** Save atomically commits validated global width, height, and interval; invalid input disables Save; Cancel discards edits; failed Save preserves the previous configuration and offers Retry/Cancel.
- **Lists and accessibility:** Session/Bank lists sort stably case-insensitively, support pointer wheel/trackpad scrolling and draggable scrollbars, and retain primary actions in empty states. Keyboard entry is limited to text/numeric fields; full keyboard navigation, screen-reader semantics, and alternative input are explicitly out of scope for this mouse-first release.
- **Failures and recovery:** Recoverable failures use canonical specific copy with Retry/Cancel; fatal startup database failure uses Acknowledge-to-exit; damaged records remain preserved and disabled when identifiable.
- **Verification:** Confirmed limits are 512 sessions and 2048 Bank figures. Internal budgets are 16 ms for default-frame work, 250 ms for maximum-field generation, and 250 ms for default-session save plus preview. Contrast, focus, line-style, textual-cue, modal click-through, target-size, and DPI evidence is required on both platforms.
